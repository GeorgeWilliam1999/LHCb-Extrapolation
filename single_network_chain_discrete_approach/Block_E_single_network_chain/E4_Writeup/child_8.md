Four figures, one per state component. Each has sixteen panels, one for every network, laid out with the step count down the page and the stage count across it. Every panel shows the **endpoint** error, the network applied $`N`$ times from the track's real state carried to $`z_0`$ and compared with the reference track at the first fibre-tracker plane, against the track's momentum, on the 1,452 test tracks. Each hexagon is coloured by how many tracks fall in it, on a logarithmic scale, and the red line is the median per momentum bin. All sixteen are at their checkpoints of 18 September 2026.
The slope labels read "mrad": as stated in the method, $`t_x`$ and $`t_y`$ are dimensionless slopes and the quantity plotted is a slope difference multiplied by $`10^3`$, which is within 7 per cent of milliradians at the edge of the acceptance and within 1 per cent for most tracks.

The shape is the same in all sixteen panels of all four figures: a steep fall from 1 GeV to about 10 GeV, a shallow minimum, and a rise again above 20 GeV that is clearest in $`x`$ and $`t_x`$, the bending plane. The numbers behind these figures are in `E3_Analysis/results/error_vs_p.csv`.
